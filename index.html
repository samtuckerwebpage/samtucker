Stay smart

Quality lodging options that match your travel style

🏨

Hotels

Best for convenience, reliable amenities, and easy airport or city-center access.

  • Look for boutique properties with breakfast included
  • Choose neighborhoods near transit or the beach
  • Book 3–6 weeks ahead for better mid-range deals
🏡

Airbnbs

Ideal when you want more space, a kitchen, or a local neighborhood feel.

  • Search for host ratings above 4.8 and recent reviews
  • Prioritize central neighborhoods over luxury views
  • Check cleaning fees and transit access before booking
💡

Best-value move

Travel during shoulder season for better prices without losing great weather.

  • Opt for shoulder season instead of peak holiday weeks
  • Use free cancellation when possible
  • Prioritize location over extra hotel amenities
